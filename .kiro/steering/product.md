# Product Overview

Personal portfolio website for Aditya, a Full Stack Developer (Backend-Oriented) based in Jakarta, Indonesia.

## Purpose

Showcase professional experience, skills, projects, certifications, education, and services. Serves as a digital resume and contact point for potential clients/employers.

## Key Sections

- Hero landing page with animated intro
- Skills (frontend, backend, database, tools)
- Work experience timeline
- Project showcase
- Education history
- Certificates gallery
- Services offered
- Contact information

## Language

UI text is primarily in Indonesian (Bahasa Indonesia). Content like certifications and technical terms remain in English.
